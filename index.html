What it counts

  • Schengen 90/180, with the Commission’s planning method and checks for C visas (validity, days on the sticker, entries).
  • US Substantial Presence Test with the 1, 1/3, 1/6 weights, kept exact, and a Form 8840 reminder.
  • US state 183-day tests: New York, Massachusetts, Minnesota and the California 9-month presumption.
  • UK Statutory Residence Test: the midnight rule, automatic tests, ties and the 60-day exceptional cap.
  • Canada: the 183-day sojourning rule and Ontario’s 153 days for OHIP. Other provinces can be set up as your own rule.
  • Spain, Portugal, Italy, Türkiye day tests, the Dutch 8-month BRP rule, and your own rule for anything else.

Why the count holds up

You enter the days

One tap for “here today” or “same as yesterday”, two taps for a range like 3 Nov to 10 Apr. No GPS, so no missed days and no invented trips.

Every number shows its working

Tap a count to see which window was used, which days counted and why, and the date it changes next.

Unknown stays unknown

A day with no record is marked Unknown, never guessed. Every day keeps an edit history: when it was entered, and what it was before.
